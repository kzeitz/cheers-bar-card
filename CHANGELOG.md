# Changelog

The version is shown at the bottom of the Bar Card. Bump `VERSION` in `source/card/bar_card_template.html` on each release.

## v0.9.0 beta – 27 Nov 2026
First version for league use (Monday 30 Nov 2026).

- Cricket, 501 doubles, 501 singles and 701 results in game order, each with "How it's calculated".
- Spot and marks lookup charts with tonight's row highlighted.
- Singles: 501 × (lower 3DA ÷ higher 3DA), per the DartConnect worksheet.
- New players: enter name, 3DA (10–100) and MPR (0.10–6.00); no automatic "assumed strong" handicap.
- HOME/AWAY labels, captain marked (C), full names, light/dark mode, phone layout.
- Throwing order ('option') note and definition; SISO labels; makeups use their scheduled night's handicaps.
- Stats through Mon 23 Nov 2026 (2 nights).
