# @Cheers Bar Card

A phone-friendly card for the **Cheers Monday Doubles Dart League** (Monday Mixed Doubles, Winter/Spring 2026/27, SISO).

Pick the night and your matchup to see:

- 501 doubles start (team-average 3DA spot chart)
- Cricket free marks (combined MPR chart)
- 501 singles starts for games 4, 6, 8 and 10 (lower 3DA / higher 3DA x 501)
- Each step of the math under "How it's worked out"

Stats come from the DartConnect league leaderboard. Makeup matches use the handicaps from the night they were scheduled.
