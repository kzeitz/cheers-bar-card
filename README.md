# @Cheers Bar Card

A phone-friendly card for the **Cheers Monday Doubles Dart League** (Monday Mixed Doubles, Winter/Spring 2026/27, SISO).

Pick the night and your matchup to see:

- Cricket free marks (combined MPR chart)
- 501 doubles start (team-average 3DA spot chart)
- 501 singles starts for games 4, 6, 8 and 10 (501 × lower 3DA ÷ higher 3DA)
- Each step of the math under "How it's calculated", with the spot and marks charts

Stats come from the DartConnect league leaderboard. New players without league stats need their 3DA and MPR entered. Makeup matches use the handicaps from the night they were scheduled.
