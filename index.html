The rules in brief

501 doubles: each team's average 3DA is (Player 1 + Player 2) ÷ 2. The difference sets the spot: under 3.0 none, then 25 points per 3.0, up to 175. The lower team starts at 501 minus the spot.
Cricket: each team's MPR is Player 1 + Player 2 (added). The difference sets free marks: under 0.50 none, then 1 mark per 0.50, up to 5. Free marks count toward closing only and don't count in anyone's MPR.
Singles: lower 3DA ÷ higher 3DA gives a percentage. The lower player starts at 501 × that percentage, rounded. The higher player starts at 501.
A player with fewer than 9 legs of stats is treated as a strong player: the league's top-quarter 3DA and MPR. Their singles are scratch.
Makeup matches use the handicaps from the night they were scheduled.
